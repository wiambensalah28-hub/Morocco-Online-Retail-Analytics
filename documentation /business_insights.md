# Business Insights

## 1. Electronics is the primary revenue driver

Electronics generates the highest revenue among the three main product
categories, making it the primary contributor to overall sales.

However, Electronics does not generate the highest profit.

**Business implication:**  
Revenue leadership does not necessarily translate into profitability
leadership.

---

## 2. Furniture is the most profitable category

Furniture generates the highest profit despite having lower revenue than
Electronics.

This highlights a significant difference between sales performance and
profitability.

**Business implication:**  
Furniture represents a strong opportunity for profitable growth.

---

## 3. Revenue and profitability vary across cities

The analysis shows significant differences in financial performance
between cities. While some cities generate substantial revenue, others
produce relatively low or negative profit.

**Business implication:**  
Geographic performance should be evaluated using both revenue and
profitability rather than sales alone.

---

## 4. Several cities are loss-making

Bechrchid, Tiznit, Essaouira and Taza show negative profitability in the
analysis.

**Business implication:**  
These markets require further investigation into pricing, product mix,
logistics and operating costs.

---

## 5. Revenue is geographically concentrated

Marrakech emerges as the leading city by revenue, while other cities
contribute smaller shares of overall sales.

**Business implication:**  
A relatively concentrated revenue base creates opportunities to strengthen
high-performing markets while developing weaker ones selectively.

---

## 6. Sales performance changes throughout the year

Monthly revenue varies considerably, with particularly strong performance
during January and March and weaker periods during parts of the year.

**Business implication:**  
The business can use historical seasonality to improve commercial and
operational planning.

---

## 7. High revenue does not always mean high profitability

The analysis reveals a recurring distinction between revenue generation
and profit generation.

Electronics leads revenue, while Furniture leads profit. Similarly,
certain cities generate sales but produce weak or negative profit.

**Business implication:**  
Revenue alone is not sufficient to evaluate business performance.
