# Business Recommendations

## 1. Improve Electronics profitability

### Finding
Electronics is the leading revenue-generating category but does not
generate the highest profit.

### Recommendation
Review pricing, supplier costs, discounts and product-level margins within
the Electronics category.

Identify high-revenue products with weak margins and prioritize actions
that improve their profitability.

### Expected impact
Increase profit contribution while protecting Electronics' strong revenue
position.

---

## 2. Expand the Furniture category

### Finding
Furniture generates the highest profit despite lower revenue than
Electronics.

### Recommendation
Increase commercial focus on profitable Furniture products through
targeted promotions, cross-selling and improved product visibility.

### Expected impact
Increase overall profitability and encourage growth in a high-value
category.

---

## 3. Investigate loss-making cities

### Finding
Several cities generate negative profit.

### Recommendation
Conduct a city-level profitability review covering:

- Logistics and delivery costs
- Product mix
- Pricing
- Discounts
- Order size
- Operational costs

For consistently loss-making markets, management should determine whether
the issue can be corrected or whether resources should be reallocated.

### Expected impact
Reduce geographic losses and improve overall profit margin.

---

## 4. Strengthen high-performing cities

### Finding
Marrakech is the leading city by revenue and represents an important
market for the business.

### Recommendation
Prioritize customer retention, product availability and targeted
marketing in high-performing cities.

### Expected impact
Increase revenue from markets that already demonstrate strong demand.

---

## 5. Incorporate seasonality into planning

### Finding
Revenue fluctuates significantly throughout the year.

### Recommendation
Use historical monthly performance to support:

- Inventory planning
- Marketing campaigns
- Sales targets
- Promotional timing
- Resource allocation

### Expected impact
Better match business resources with expected demand.

---

## 6. Make profitability a core performance metric

### Finding
The highest-revenue category or city is not necessarily the most
profitable.

### Recommendation
Evaluate categories, cities and products using both revenue and
profitability KPIs.

Key metrics should include:

- Revenue
- Profit
- Profit Margin
- Profit per Unit
- Revenue per Unit
- Profit by City
- Profit by Category

### Expected impact
Encourage sustainable growth rather than focusing solely on sales volume.
